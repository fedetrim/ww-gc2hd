2nd save is the entrence of getting the master sword

3rd save is after ganon and getting the fire and ice arrows

4th save map complete but not dungones 

5th save is after the earth temple is beaten

7th save is after all triforce pieces are collected

9th saved is before puppet ganon

10th save is the end
